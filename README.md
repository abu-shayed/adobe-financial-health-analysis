## Question
Is Adobe financially healthy, and will it stay that way?

## Data
Adobe 10-K filings (SEC EDGAR). Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, FY2021-FY2025.

## Method
11 ratios (liquidity, leverage, profitability, cash quality) built in Excel, trend verdict per ratio, Power BI dashboard.

## Key finding
Strong business, shrinking cushion.
Current ratio fell from 1.35 to 1.00, 
debt-to-equity rose from 0.28 to 0.54 
 Buybacks exceeded free cash flow in FY2024 and FY2025. Margins and cash conversion stayed strong.
